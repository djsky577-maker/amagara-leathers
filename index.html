— The Process

Slow made.
Built to be repaired.

True luxury isn't mass-produced. Every Amagara piece is cut, skived, and saddle-stitched by hand. We don't use plastic edge paints or synthetic linings — just pure leather, waxed thread, and solid brass that gets better with time.

  • 01

    Vegetable-Tanned Hides

    Durable, breathable, and develops a rich patina over time.

  • 02

    Saddle Stitching

    Two needles, one thread. If a stitch breaks, the rest hold tight.

  • 03

    Solid Brass Hardware

    Cast, not stamped. Solid weight, zero flaking or peeling.

  • 04

    Lifetime Care

    Send it back anytime for re-stitching, conditioning, or repair.
